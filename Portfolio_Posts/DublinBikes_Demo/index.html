About this map

The map shows the reported availability of Dublin Bikes at 15-minute intervals on the selected day. Each frame uses the latest station report recorded within that interval.

How to read the stations

Circle size reflects the number of bikes available. Color shows the balance between bikes and usable empty docks. Red stations have no bikes to rent; blue stations have no available docks for returns.

“Working capacity” is the number of available bikes plus available docks at that moment. It may be lower than the station’s stated capacity when equipment is unavailable.

Neighborhood layers

Residential density provides nighttime population context. Daytime density is based on 2016 data and helps indicate where people are present during working hours.
